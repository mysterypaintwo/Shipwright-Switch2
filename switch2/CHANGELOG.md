# Changelog

## v1.1

**Added**
- Updated to Ship of Harkinian 9.3.1, with its full-screen menu and search.
- Gyro and right stick aiming in first person, in Enhancements > Quality of Life > Controls.
- Save and Load State buttons in the Cheats menu. Map them, or a button combo, in Settings > Controls.
- A loading screen with a progress bar.
- A reminder at startup that - opens the menu.

**Improved**
- Much less stutter when changing maps and in cutscenes. Shaders are prepared during boot.
- The menu is larger and easier to read and touch.
- Clearing or resetting controls no longer freezes the game.
- The quit option is now called Quit Game.

**Fixed**
- Audio crackling and playback problems.
- The controls menu now shows Nintendo button names.
- The yellow dirt path in Hyrule Field no longer cuts off in front of the player.
- Moving the right stick while aiming no longer leaves first person.
- Missing game files now show a clear message. Press A to return to the Homebrew Menu.

**Updating:** your old `oot.otr` no longer works. Make a new `oot.o2r` with `GAME-DATA.md`, delete `soh.otr` and `oot.otr`, and back up `/switch/soh/Save/` first. The first launch takes longer while it prepares shaders.

## v1.0

Initial release, based on Ship of Harkinian 8.0.6.
